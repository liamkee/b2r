Title: Separate business registration from investment incentives

Record type: evidence

Review date: 2026-10-31; review due: 2026-11-30

Evidence period: not stated

SOURCE-SUPPORTED SUMMARY:
- Cambodia’s registration portal coordinates business, tax and labour registrations. CDC describes a separate investment-project registration route for eligible projects under the investment regime.

ANALYST INFERENCE, NOT A SOURCE FACT: Do not represent Qualified Investment Project benefits as automatic for every newly registered startup.

UNKNOWNS:
- Legal form, ownership/land restrictions, sector permissions and eligibility for investment incentives require review.
