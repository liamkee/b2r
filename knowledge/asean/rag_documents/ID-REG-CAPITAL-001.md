Title: Paid-up capital is not project investment or revenue

Evidence period: 2025 regulatory change

SOURCE-SUPPORTED SUMMARY:
- UNCTAD reports that BKPM Regulation 5/2025 reduced the general paid-up capital requirement for a foreign-owned limited liability company from IDR10 billion to IDR2.5 billion.

ANALYST INFERENCE, NOT A SOURCE FACT: Use as a verification trigger only. Never compare annual sales with a paid-up capital requirement to declare eligibility.
