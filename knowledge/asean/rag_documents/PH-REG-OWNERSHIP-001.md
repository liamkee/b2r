Evidence period: not stated

SOURCE-SUPPORTED SUMMARY:
- Executive Order 113 of 13 April 2026 promulgates the Thirteenth Regular Foreign Investment Negative List, reserving listed activities subject to stated exceptions and conditions. Government business guidance also distinguishes business, tax, local and sector registrations.

ANALYST INFERENCE, NOT A SOURCE FACT: Use the 13th list rather than an old 12th-list summary. Do not apply a universal 40% foreign-equity cap to all businesses.

UNKNOWNS:
- Activity-specific ownership restrictions, domestic-market capital conditions, nationality and establishment form are not determined.
